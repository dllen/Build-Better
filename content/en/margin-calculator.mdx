## What is a margin calculator?

A margin calculator helps sellers determine the actual profit margin on a product after accounting for cost and selling price.

## How to use

1. Enter the product cost
2. Enter the selling price
3. The calculator shows margin %, profit amount, and markup

## Why it matters

Knowing your margin helps you price competitively without leaving profit on the table.
