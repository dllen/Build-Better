# Hijri Calendar Converter

Convert between Gregorian and Islamic (Hijri) calendar dates. Includes Ramadan estimates.

## How to Use

Enter any Gregorian date to convert it to the Islamic Hijri calendar, or vice versa. The tool provides accurate date conversions based on the Umm al-Qura calendar, with estimated Ramadan timing.

## Key Features

- Bidirectional conversion between Gregorian and Hijri calendars
- Ramadan timing estimates with Hijri month highlighting
- Support for both Um al-Qura and tabular calendar methods
- Automatic adjustment for leap years in both calendars

## FAQ


### How accurate is the Hijri date conversion?

This tool uses the Umm al-Qura calendar, which is the official civil calendar of Saudi Arabia. It provides high accuracy for modern dates, though religious dates may vary by +/- 1 day depending on moon sighting.

### What is the difference between Islamic year and Hijri year?

They are the same. The Hijri calendar began on July 16, 622 CE (Gregorian) with the Prophet Muhammad's migration from Mecca to Medina. Each Hijri year is approximately 354 days long.

### Can I convert a date range for multiple days?

Currently the tool converts single dates. For bulk conversions, you can use the tool repeatedly or export results for calendar integration.
