## FAQ


### Why do different payment methods have different fees?

Fees vary based on settlement speed, fraud risk, transaction type, and market competition. Card payments typically cost more due to interchange fees, while bank transfers are often cheaper.

### Are these fees updated regularly?

Payment processor fees change frequently. This tool provides estimates based on publicly available rates - always verify with the payment provider for accurate quotes.

### Can I negotiate lower fees?

Yes, many payment processors offer volume discounts or custom pricing for high-volume merchants. Contact their sales teams for negotiated rates.
