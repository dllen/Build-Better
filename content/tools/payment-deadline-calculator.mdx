## FAQ


### What do payment terms like Net 30 mean?

Net 30 means payment is due 30 days from the invoice date. "Net" simply means the full amount is due. Other terms like 2/10 Net 30 offer a 2% discount if paid within 10 days.

### How do EOM terms work?

EOM (End of Month) terms mean the due date is based on the end of the invoice month, not the invoice date. For example, an invoice dated Jan 15 with Net 30 EOM would be due Feb 28/29.

### What is 15th MF?

15th MF (15th of the Following Month) is common in Southeast Asia. The due date is always the 15th of the month after the invoice month, regardless of when the invoice was issued.
